Privacy Policy

Reverie: A Music-Driven Emotional Journal App

Last updated: 12 May 2026

Reverie is developed by Theodore Graham as an independent project. This privacy policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and how it is protected.

What Data We Collect

Reverie collects and stores the following data that you provide directly through your use of the app:

Account information including your email address, username, date of birth, and profile photo. Emotional journal entries including your selected emotions, attached songs, written reflections, photographs, voice notes, and tags. App preferences including theme selections, reminder settings, and onboarding state.

How Your Data Is Stored

All data is stored locally on your device using SQLite as the primary data store. When you are connected to the internet, your data is synchronised to Google Firebase (Firestore and Firebase Storage) as a cloud backup. Data in transit is encrypted using TLS. Firestore security rules restrict access so that only you can read or write your own data.

Authentication

Reverie uses Firebase Authentication for account management. You can sign in with email and password or via Google Sign-In (OAuth 2.0). Reverie does not store your password directly. Authentication is handled entirely by Firebase.

Music Data

When you search for songs, Reverie queries third-party music APIs (Spotify, iTunes, and Deezer) to retrieve song metadata such as title, artist, album name, and artwork URL. Reverie does not access your streaming accounts, playlists, or listening history. Only the metadata for songs you select is stored.

What We Do Not Collect

Reverie does not collect location data, device identifiers, analytics or tracking data, advertising data, or any data not directly related to the features you use. Reverie does not share your data with any third parties. Reverie does not display advertisements.

Your Rights

You have full control over your data at all times. You can export all of your data in JSON format from within the app via the Privacy and Data page. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the app. These features support your rights under the UK General Data Protection Regulation and the Data Protection Act 2018, including the right of access and the right to erasure.

Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, all data stored in Firebase (including Firestore documents and media files in Firebase Storage) is permanently removed. Local data on your device is cleared when the app is uninstalled.

Children

Reverie is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided data through the app, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or your data, please contact us at reveriejournalapp@gmail.com.
